    Founder Tip Tuesday

    Mutiny got 30 yeses in an hour

    Nobody at Mutiny had time set aside to deliver what the offer promised. Matt Ratchford paused the campaign and built every piece himself.

    Mutiny's sales team

    One account executive, nobody booking meetings

    The offer was custom sales material for each prospect, built with Mutiny's own product. Sending it to everyone meant spending those hours on accounts that weren't a priority.

    Matt Ratchford, Mutiny

    Logos build credibility

    At Mutiny's stage the thing to build is credibility with the next buyer. It comes from great logos on the website and the stories of how those customers use the product.

    The cut

    Keep the logos worth showing

    400 accounts

    Mutiny's top-priority list.

    214 accounts

    The companies whose logos he would want on the website. Personal outreach. Everyone else gets a free sign-up with credits.
